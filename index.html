01 · Developer explainer · spec piece

Why one database hits a wall

Source on GitHub ↗
Format
66 s, 16:9 · 35 s vertical cut · 2 thumbnails
Role
Script, motion, sound, edit
Built with
Remotion, Kokoro TTS, Whisper, Python, Claude
Speed
First full cut in ~40 min. One script line changed, re-voiced and re-timed in 2 min 26 s

Decisions

  • Subject on screen in the first second: “1 database.” A frame check caught it arriving at 2.2 s, so I moved it.
  • Picture and sound read one cue sheet, so the database splits on the word “split”, with the sound.
  • Narration is re-transcribed by Whisper to catch mispronunciations and to time captions word by word.
Vertical cut, generated from the same source.
Making-of: the pipeline, one-line change before/after, machine QA, fixes caught on stills.

02 · Live action · travel vlog

Mt. Daruma, Izu

Format
55 s, 16:9, English subtitles from French dialogue
Source
35 min of handheld footage from one day
Role
Shot (with my wife), story, edit, sound, score

What stayed, what went

  • Built around one honest line: after a year in Japan, she needed a reset. Everything else serves it.
  • Cold open on the payoff (“It’s like a movie.”), closed on its echo at sunset.
  • L-cuts carry her monologue over the climb, hiding the jump cuts.
  • Score written to length: its last chord lands on the final shot and fades out with the picture.

How I work

AI does the repetition. I make the calls.

Story first

Find the one line or image the piece is about, and cut toward it.

Rough cut fast

Transcribe everything, pull selects and timings by script, assemble in minutes.

Taste pass

Pacing, what to cut, where to breathe, sound. This part stays human.

Check by machine

Contact sheets of key frames, loudness, and speech checks before anything ships.
